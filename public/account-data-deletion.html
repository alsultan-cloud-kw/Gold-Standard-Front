Account & data deletion

For the Gold Standard mobile app and website (goldstandardkw.com). Effective date: April 8, 2026.

You can request deletion of your Gold Standard account and associated personal data, including related communication records we control.

1. How to request deletion

Email info@goldstandardkw.com with the subject line Data Deletion Request.

Include your full name, registered email and/or phone number, and any details needed to verify account ownership.

You may request:

  • full account deletion, or
  • deletion of some or all personal data without closing the account (state clearly what you want removed).

2. Verification

For security, we may ask for additional verification before processing. If we cannot verify ownership, we may not complete the request.

3. Timeline

After verification, we process deletion within a reasonable period — typically within 30 days — unless a longer retention period is required by law.

4. What gets deleted

  • Account profile details maintained in this application
  • Non-essential communication records linked to your account
  • Other personal data that is not legally required to be retained

5. What may be retained

We may retain certain records where required for legal, tax, accounting, security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or regulatory compliance (for example transaction/invoice records required under Kuwait law).

6. Meta and WhatsApp data

If your request relates to WhatsApp or Meta platform data, we process deletion for data controlled by our application. Data controlled directly by Meta may also be subject to Meta’s own deletion processes.
